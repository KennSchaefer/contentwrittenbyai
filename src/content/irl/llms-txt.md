> Have clients or colleagues asked you about llms.txt? What did they expect it to do, and what did you tell them?

We brought them information about llms.txt after the first public mentions of it with the note that it was of low priority. That is still the message, but there is a bit of a lean towards the possibility of it becoming more important.

> Have you added llms.txt to any site you work on, and did you see anything afterward, in logs, AI citations or traffic? If you haven't, why not?

Currently, no.  Primarily because there isn’t evidence that Google nor OpenAI care about it so why put resources against its development and upkeep.

> When a new "do this for AI search" tactic shows up, how do you decide whether it's worth a client's time?

It does vary a little client to client.  The new tactics are brought to them with a recommendation based on whether or not their visibility or clicks will improve.  I usually have an understanding of their resources and priorities so that will play into the recommendation somewhat.  I also know if there are other SEO priorities that will be more impactful.  All to say, it varies and prioritization is key.
